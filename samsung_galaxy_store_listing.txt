FULL DESCRIPTION (4,000 characters max)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Create a professional resume with confidence using AI Resume Builder — a
private, offline-first career toolkit for building resumes, preparing for
interviews, writing cover letters, and managing your job search.

Build as many resumes as you need. Add your profile, professional summary,
experience, education, skills, projects, certifications, awards, volunteer
work, languages, publications, and custom sections. Choose a professional
template, edit your content, preview your resume, and export or share it when
you are ready.

AI CAREER TOOLS
----------------
• AI resume review and scoring
• Cover letter generation from your resume and a job description
• Interview preparation for HR, technical, and behavioral questions
• Career Coach chat for practical career guidance
• Portfolio bio and project content generation
• Resume, profile, and job-application writing assistance

YOUR COMPLETE CAREER WORKSPACE
------------------------------
• Resume Builder — create and manage multiple resumes
• Templates — choose a style for your target role
• Job Tracker — track companies, positions, statuses, dates, offers, and notes
• Analytics — review resume progress, AI usage, exports, and activity
• Favorites — keep important resumes close at hand
• Search — quickly find resumes, jobs, and saved content
• Backup & Restore — keep a local copy of your career data

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
-----------------
No online account is required. Resume, profile, favorites, job-tracker, and
settings data are stored locally on your device. AI features use your own
Groq API key, which is stored securely in the app's encrypted preferences.
Your key is never included in the app or committed to the project.

PERSONALIZED EXPERIENCE
-----------------------
Enjoy a modern Material Design 3 interface with light, dark, AMOLED, and
system themes. The layout supports portrait and landscape orientations, with
clear empty states, progress indicators, and accessible controls.

HOW IT WORKS
------------
1. Create your profile and start a resume.
2. Add sections such as experience, education, skills, and projects.
3. Choose a template and preview your resume.
4. Use AI tools to review content, write a cover letter, or prepare for an
   interview.
5. Export, share, favorite, or continue editing your work.

REQUIREMENTS
------------
A free Groq API key and internet connection are required for AI generation
only. Local resume editing, searching, favorites, job tracking, and backup
features work without an account and are designed to work offline.
