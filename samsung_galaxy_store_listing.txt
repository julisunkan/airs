FULL DESCRIPTION (4,000 characters max)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Struggling to find the right words for your emails? AI Email
Writer takes the stress out of professional communication by
generating polished, context-aware emails in seconds — powered
by Groq's lightning-fast llama-3.3-70b-versatile AI model.

Whether you're drafting a job application, following up with a
client, writing a customer support reply, or sending a formal
business proposal, AI Email Writer crafts the perfect message
every time. Just describe what you need, choose your tone and
style, and let the AI do the writing.

HOW IT WORKS
─────────────
1. Enter the recipient and purpose of your email
2. Choose the tone, type, length, and language
3. Tap "Generate Email" — your professional email is ready
4. Edit the subject or body directly if you want tweaks
5. Save, copy, or share with one tap

POWERFUL CUSTOMISATION
───────────────────────
Every email is different. That's why AI Email Writer gives you
full control over how your message sounds and reads:

• 10 Tones — Professional, Friendly, Formal, Casual,
  Persuasive, Empathetic, Assertive, Diplomatic, Enthusiastic,
  and Concise
• 20+ Email Types — Business, Job Application, Sales, Customer
  Support, Follow-Up, Thank You, Apology, Invitation, Academic,
  Cold Outreach, and more
• 3 Lengths — Short, Medium, or Long to match the occasion
• 9 Languages — English, Spanish, French, German, Italian,
  Portuguese, Arabic, Chinese, and Japanese

YOUR EMAILS, YOUR WAY
──────────────────────
AI Email Writer doesn't just generate — it lets you take full
ownership of every message. Tap directly on the subject or body
to edit any part of a generated email before saving. Go back
to any saved email later and edit it again from your History.
Nothing is locked; every word is yours to change.

YOUR DATA STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE
────────────────────────────────
All generated emails are stored privately in a local database
on your phone — no cloud accounts, no sign-ups, no data
harvesting. Your API key is stored securely in the app's
private storage and never shared.

BEAUTIFUL, COLOURFUL INTERFACE
────────────────────────────────
Enjoy a vibrant Material Design 3 interface featuring a rich
earth-tone palette of greens, oranges, yellows, browns, and
reds. Dark mode is supported automatically, and the layout
adapts to both portrait and landscape orientations.

STAY ORGANISED
───────────────
• Email History — browse every email you've generated
• Favourites — star your best emails for quick access
• Search — instantly find any email by recipient, subject,
  purpose, or content
• Sort — view emails newest-first or oldest-first

REQUIREMENTS
─────────────
A free Groq API key is required for email generation. Sign up
at console.groq.com — it takes under two minutes and the free
tier is generous. Enter your key once in Settings and you're
ready to generate.
